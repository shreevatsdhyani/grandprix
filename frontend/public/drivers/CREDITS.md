# Driver portrait credits

`frontend/public/drivers/*.jpg` are freely-licensed photographs from Wikimedia
Commons, downscaled to 520px and re-encoded as JPEG. They ship in the repo so the
demo runs with no network.

No official Formula 1 or team press imagery is used. Regenerate with
`backend/scripts/fetch_portraits.py`, which refuses to download anything whose
Commons licence is not clearly free.
